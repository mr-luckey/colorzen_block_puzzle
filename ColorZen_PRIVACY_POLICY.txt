The App uses local storage (Hive) on your device to save:
- Game progress and saved sessions (Classic / Daily / Zen)
- Scores, best scores, and ranking history
- Settings (for example: sound, music, haptics, theme)
- Daily challenge progress for the current day

This information stays on your device. In the current version, we do not transmit this local gameplay data to AppWareTech servers.

3.2 Information we do NOT collect ourselves
We do not intentionally collect:
- Name, email address, phone number, or postal address (unless you email us for support)
- Precise location

- Government ID or payment / financial details
